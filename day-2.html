Fieldwork & Methods

How do we turn a research question into a practical, repeatable field method? This day focuses on choosing what to observe, how to sample it, and what different methods can—and cannot—tell us.

MODULE 01

From question to field method

Identify the information needed, sampling unit and method that best fit the question.

MODULE 02

Observing birds in the field

Observation, surveys, field records and practical constraints.

MODULE 03

Sampling avian behaviour

Sampling rules, time, space, observer effects and consistency.

MODULE 04

Studying bird migration

Field observations, ringing, tracking and monitoring approaches.

MODULE 05

Choosing and evaluating methods

Compare strengths, limitations, ethics and feasibility.
