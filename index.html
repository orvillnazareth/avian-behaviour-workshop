THE BIG IDEA 01 / 03

From watching
to understanding.

Birds offer extraordinary opportunities to explore animal behaviour: from foraging and communication to courtship, parental care and long-distance migration.

This workshop connects the questions we ask about birds with the practical methods researchers use to investigate them. The emphasis is on clear observation, thoughtful study design and interpreting evidence carefully.
